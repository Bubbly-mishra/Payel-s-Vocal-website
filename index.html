About your teacher

Hi, I'm Payel — musician, vocalist & keyboard teacher.

A passionate and experienced singer and vocal music teacher specializing in a wide range of Indian music traditions. She teaches Bollywood songs, Rabindra Sangeet, Nazrul Geeti, Bengali folk music, and various other Bengali songs, helping students develop both their vocal skills and their understanding of the music.

Her teaching focuses on voice training, sur (pitch), taal (rhythm), pronunciation, expression, and confidence, making the learning process enjoyable and accessible for beginners as well as experienced singers.

Whether you want to learn Bollywood melodies, explore the richness of Bengali music, or develop a strong foundation in singing, her personalized guidance can help you become a more confident and expressive vocalist.

What you'll learn

Everything you need to actually sing and play — not just theory.

Each lesson plan is personalized, but here's the ground every student covers on the way to performing confidently.

Vocal Training

Breath control, pitch, and tone across the styles you love — Rabindra Sangeet, Nazrul Geeti, Bollywood, and folk — built on solid classical fundamentals.

Keyboard Technique

Hand position, finger independence, and posture that builds a foundation you won't need to unlearn later — whether you're starting fresh or fixing old habits.

Music Fundamentals

Reading notation, understanding scales and chords, and training your ear to recognize what you hear — the vocabulary that makes every other skill click faster.

Songs & Repertoire

Learn pieces you actually want to sing or play — chosen with you, not assigned to you — so practice stays motivating from your very first weeks.

Rhythm & Timing

Steady tempo, clapping and counting exercises, and singing or playing along with a metronome until timing becomes second nature.

Practical Performance Skills

Sight-reading, playing or singing by ear, and simple improvisation — the real-world skills that turn a student into a performer.
