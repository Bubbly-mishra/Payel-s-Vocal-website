About your teacher

Hi, I'm Payel — musician and keyboard teacher.

I teach keyboard and music the way I wish I'd been taught: patiently, practically, and built around what you actually want to play. Whether you're picking up a keyboard for the very first time or coming back to sharpen skills you already have, lessons start from where you are — not from a rigid syllabus.

My approach blends solid fundamentals — reading music, understanding rhythm, training your ear — with the songs and styles that made you want to learn in the first place. You'll build real, practical playing skills you can use, not just exercises you'll forget.

What you'll learn

Everything you need to actually play — not just theory.

Each lesson plan is personalized, but here's the ground every student covers on the way to playing confidently.

Keyboard Technique

Hand position, finger independence, and posture that builds a foundation you won't need to unlearn later — whether you're starting fresh or fixing old habits.

Music Fundamentals

Reading notation, understanding scales and chords, and training your ear to recognize what you hear — the vocabulary that makes every other skill click faster.

Songs & Repertoire

Learn pieces you actually want to play — chosen with you, not assigned to you — so practice stays motivating from your very first weeks.

Rhythm & Timing

Steady tempo, clapping and counting exercises, and playing along with a metronome until timing becomes second nature instead of a constant struggle.

Practical Playing Skills

Sight-reading new pieces, playing by ear, improvising simple accompaniments — the real-world skills that turn a student into a musician.
